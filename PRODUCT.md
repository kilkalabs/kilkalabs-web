# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Audience priority, confirmed by the owner:
1. People discovering KilkaLabs' apps.
2. Existing app users seeking support.
3. Prospective service clients.

## Product Purpose

Introduce KilkaLabs' apps and help visitors discover them; provide a route to support for existing users. Client inquiries are secondary to both.

## Positioning

Public attribution must be “Made by KilkaLabs.” Protect the owners' privacy: do not publish personal identities, family relationships, or the private team composition. No distinctive competitive claim is established; do not invent one or imply a larger team.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing implementation: a single-page Astro website with custom CSS and vanilla JavaScript, statically built for kilkalabs.com.
- Current entry point: `src/pages/index.astro`; styling: `src/styles/custom.css`; interactions: `src/scripts/main.js`.
- The existing page links to app information, an App Store listing, and email contacts. These are implementation evidence, not newly confirmed product facts.
- The owner considers all current copy outdated and permits rewriting it. Existing service claims, location, support response times, app descriptions, and contact details must not be treated as confirmed promises.
- Confirmed lineup: In The Mood (`https://getinthemood.app/`), Letterwright (`https://letterwright.kilkalabs.com/`), and upcoming Workmark (`https://workmark.kilkalabs.com/`, website already live).
- App discovery should lead primarily to each app's website, with a secondary direct download link for released apps.
- Confirmed contacts: `support@kilkalabs.com` for support and `info@kilkalabs.com` for general inquiries.
- Product-site evidence checked during implementation: In The Mood's official site describes its private couples app and links to App Store ID 6744419061; Letterwright's official site describes neighbouring-letter swaps, no timer, no ads, no account, and links to App Store ID 6811364562; Workmark's official site confirms chosen-office-Wi-Fi recording, local storage, macOS 14+, and coming-soon status. These are sourced app claims, not claims inferred from legacy company copy.
- Open decisions: services still offered and support expectations. Neither is promised on the new homepage.

## Brand Commitments

KilkaLabs is the public identity; use “Made by KilkaLabs,” never personal or family attribution. The owner requests a new visual identity for the homepage rather than refinement of the incumbent look.

## Evidence on Hand

- Official sources: https://getinthemood.app/, https://letterwright.kilkalabs.com/, https://workmark.kilkalabs.com/.
- Homepage product imagery downloaded from those sources: `public/images/in-the-mood.webp`, `public/images/letterwright.webp`, `public/images/workmark.webp`. Origin sidecars accompany each. Letterwright is its official gameplay screenshot; Workmark's preview is explicitly labeled demo data.
- Existing brand asset: `public/images/logo.webp`. The legacy `public/images/in_the_mood_1.webp` is not used by the new page.
- No testimonials, customer counts, performance benchmarks, or case-study results were established during init. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Put app discovery before support, and support before client acquisition.
- Respect owner privacy; attribute work to KilkaLabs without inventing team scale.
- Prefer concrete app facts over generic agency claims.
- Confirm outdated factual claims before publishing replacements.
